Privacy

How we look after the details you share on Joshua and Lucia’s wedding website.

This page is for the save-the-date site and the SMS reminders that go with it — Joshua and Lucia’s wedding on Saturday, 7 November 2026, at 11:00 AM.

What we collect

If you sign up for reminders or send an RSVP, we collect your name, email, and/or phone number — only what you choose to give us. We also keep a short note if you leave a message for the couple.

How we use it

We use that information only to send wedding reminders and livestream alerts you opted into, and to keep the guest list for the day. We do not sell your data, we do not share or sell phone numbers, and we do not use it for advertising.

Text messages

If you tick the SMS consent box on the reminder or RSVP form and share a phone number, SMS is sent through Twilio. Leaving a phone number without ticking that box does not sign you up for texts. You will receive wedding reminder and livestream-alert texts from Joshua and Lucia — up to 4 messages (welcome, 7 days before, 1 day before, and the day of). Message and data rates may apply. You can leave the list at any time by replying STOP.

Questions
